# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JavaScript

## Users

Students planning and organizing their study work, typically as individuals managing their own daily priorities.

## Product Purpose

Today helps students capture and organize the study tasks in front of them. Success means making task listing easy and making planning feel enjoyable, calm, and close to a journaling ritual.

## Positioning

The product is positioned around low-friction daily task capture with a calm, journaling-inspired experience. No distinct mechanism beyond that experience has been confirmed.

## Operating Context

The primary workflow is to open a daily workspace, add a task, review the task list, mark work complete, and filter or clear completed tasks.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The current implementation supports adding tasks, marking tasks complete, viewing task counts, filtering by all/active/completed, and clearing completed tasks.
- The intended implementation stack is plain static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
- Persistence across page reloads is undecided.
- Team collaboration and accounts are not part of the confirmed product scope.

## Evidence on Hand

- [index.html](index.html) contains the Today task-board surface and its user-facing copy.
- [script.js](script.js) contains the current in-memory task interactions.
- [styles.css](styles.css) contains the current visual implementation.
- No external product claims, customer evidence, or required brand assets were provided.

## Product Principles

- Make capturing the next study task immediate.
- Keep daily planning calm and approachable.
- Make progress visible without adding cognitive overhead.
- Preserve a focused individual workflow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific accessibility standard was established. The existing web interface should preserve semantic HTML, keyboard access, visible focus states, and readable text as it evolves.
